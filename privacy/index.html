Privacy Notice

Effective 10 November 2026 · Last updated 10 November 2026

StudyStrides (ABN 55 179 267 733) is a registered business name. This document explains, in plain English, how personal information is handled, how access works, and how refunds and cancellations work.

In short

  • We use personal information to give each student their own login, provide the Studies of Religion study site, match a payment or school entitlement to the right person, and answer questions sent to us.
  • When a student signs up, the study site uses an email address, a password, and a nickname.
  • The site may keep some study progress and settings on the device.
  • Where this is enabled, some progress and settings may also be stored with the signed-in account.
  • The study site and the public website use Microsoft Clarity to help us understand how the sites are used.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • You do not need a paid login to contact us. A friend, parent, school or other person can use the contact form or email.
  • Many StudyStrides students are under 18. We aim to collect only what we need and to explain ourselves in plain English.
  • Questions: support@studystrides.com.au or the contact form on studystrides.com.au

Who we are

StudyStrides (ABN 55 179 267 733) provides an online study site that helps NSW HSC students prepare for Studies of Religion. StudyStrides is a registered business name.

  • Public website: studystrides.com.au
  • Study site: sor.studystrides.com.au

How to contact us

You can contact us through the contact form on studystrides.com.au or by emailing support@studystrides.com.au.

You do not need a StudyStrides login or a paid account to contact us. A parent, guardian, school, friend or other person may get in touch on their own behalf or for a student.

If you write to us, we receive and use the message and any contact details you choose to give (usually an email address) so we can read the enquiry and reply. We do not require you to prove a paid-site identity before we can receive a question.

What information we collect, and why

We aim to collect only what we need to provide access and support.

When a student signs up, the study site sends the following to Supabase Auth (our sign-in service):

  • email address
  • password
  • a nickname

The password is used to create and check the login. Sign-in is handled through Supabase Auth. We do not keep the password in a student profile table.

The student profile stored in our database includes a nickname. A signed-in person can update their own profile.

If paid or arranged access is recorded, our access records can include:

  • the source of the entitlement
  • its status
  • an end time
  • Stripe identifiers when a payment is recorded
  • an administrative note in an access audit record

School-paid access, bank-transfer access and complimentary access are arranged by StudyStrides directly, not through Stripe.

When someone contacts us by form or email, we process the content of the message and any contact details they include, so we can reply. That person does not have to be a signed-in user.

If information needed for a login or paid access is not provided, we may not be able to create that access or connect a payment. A general enquiry can still be sent without a login.

What the site stores on the device

After sign-in, the site stores a signed-in session in the browser and uses a sign-in cookie so the site can recognise that the student is signed in.

The site may keep some study progress and settings on the device.

The site may also use local storage for study settings, planners, quiz progress, visit or activity markers, notification “read” state, and similar site features. Those values stay on the device unless the browser data is cleared.

Where this is enabled, some progress and settings may also be stored with the signed-in account. This notice does not say that all progress is copied to the account, or that every item stored on the device is also stored with us.

Who else may handle information

We use other companies to help us run StudyStrides. They handle information so they can provide their service. Some of them are international companies and may handle information outside Australia.

Those companies include:

  • Supabase — sign-in, database, and related backend functions
  • Netlify — building and hosting the study site
  • jsDelivr — delivers a sign-in library used by the study site
  • Google Fonts — delivers a typeface used on the study site
  • Microsoft Clarity — a usage-measurement service used on the study site and the public website (see below)
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the public website
  • FormSubmit — delivers messages sent through the contact form on the public website
  • Stripe — processes online card payments when that checkout option is used

The public website may also cause the browser to contact GitHub Pages and any other services that website uses.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use student information to build marketing lists for other businesses.

Payments

Online card payments, when that option is available, are started from the study site’s subscribe page and completed through Stripe’s hosted checkout. Stripe processes the payment details entered on Stripe’s checkout. StudyStrides receives the information needed to confirm the payment and connect it to the correct access, which can include Stripe identifiers in our access records.

School-paid arrangements, bank transfers and complimentary entitlements are administered by StudyStrides independently of Stripe.

How we understand use of the study site

The study site and the public website use Microsoft Clarity, a Microsoft usage-measurement service, to help us understand how those sites are used — for example, which pages people visit and how they move through them. When that service runs, the browser contacts Microsoft.

Clarity can use cookies or similar device and session identifiers. That information is not anonymous simply because a name is not shown with it. We do not claim that raw session recordings are anonymous, or that every input or on-screen detail is masked.

We may use aggregated usage information to understand how the study site is used overall. Where we prepare summaries that no longer identify a person or device, we may also use that de-identified information for the same purpose. This does not mean that Clarity’s underlying session data is anonymous.

Students under 18

Many StudyStrides students are under 18. A parent, guardian or school may arrange or pay for a student’s access.

Australian privacy law protects personal information regardless of age. It does not set one fixed age at which every young person can make every privacy decision. If you are a student, parent, guardian or school and you are unsure who should contact us about a student’s information, use the contact form or email support@studystrides.com.au. You do not need a paid login to do that.

Student work and automated tools

The student study site does not have confirmed artificial-intelligence features. This notice does not say that student work is sent to an AI service.

Study tools (for example planners or quiz progress) are for the student’s own use of the site.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only while we need it to provide access, keep proper records of payments and entitlements, answer questions, and meet legal record-keeping duties. Some payment and business records may need to be kept after access ends.

If you want information corrected or deleted, contact us. We will consider the request and explain if we need to keep a particular record.

Access, correction and complaints

Use the contact form or email support@studystrides.com.au if you want to:

  • ask what information we hold about you or a student you are responsible for
  • correct information that is wrong
  • ask us to delete information
  • make a privacy complaint

You do not need a paid login to make that request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can seek guidance from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or NSW Fair Trading (nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/fair-trading).

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal information in an important way, we will update this notice and, where we can, tell people who have access or who paid for access.
