Refunds and Cancellations

Effective 10 November 2026 · Last updated 10 November 2026

StudyStrides (ABN 55 179 267 733) is a registered business name. This document explains, in plain English, how personal information is handled, how access works, and how refunds and cancellations work.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

When you buy StudyStrides, you automatically have consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law. Those rights cannot be taken away by anything we say in a policy, on a website, or in an email.

In plain terms, you are entitled to expect that StudyStrides:

  • matches the description of the offer
  • is fit for the purpose of providing the described HSC Studies of Religion study access
  • is provided with due care and skill

Those rights apply to digital access just as they apply to other purchases. They do not disappear because the site is online, because a student has signed in, or because a certain number of days has passed.

We will not say “no refunds” for digital access, and we will not refuse a remedy the law requires.

If something is wrong

If there is a problem — for example, paid access is not provided, the study site is not what was described, or a serious fault cannot be fixed in a reasonable time — contact us through the contact form or at support@studystrides.com.au.

We may need to look into the problem. What we must do then depends on the law and on whether the problem is minor or major:

  • For a minor problem, we must fix it in a reasonable time. That will often mean restoring access or correcting the issue.
  • For a major problem, you are entitled to the remedies the Australian Consumer Law gives you. That can include cancelling and receiving a refund.

A refund required by the consumer guarantees should be for the amount the law requires, in the same form as the original payment unless we agree otherwise with the payer.

The Australian Consumer Law does not require a refund merely because someone changed their mind, found a different resource, or decided they no longer wanted the site — unless a change-of-mind policy applies, or another legal right applies.

Change-of-mind courtesy

In addition to your Australian Consumer Law rights, StudyStrides offers the following courtesy.

If you ask us within 14 days after the later of:

  1. the date the A$25 payment was made, or
  2. the date the student’s login was first made available

and the student has used the site only incidentally (for example, signing in to check that access works), we will refund the amount paid as a change-of-mind courtesy.

Incidental use means brief use to confirm that access works, not substantial study use of the site. We will apply this fairly. This courtesy does not limit or replace your Australian Consumer Law rights. Those rights continue after the 14 days and continue even if the site has been used. If there is a problem with the site itself, we will assess that under the consumer guarantees, not only under this courtesy.

There is no subscription and no automatic renewal, so there is no ongoing membership to cancel. A courtesy refund is a refund of the one-off payment.

A refund under this courtesy would normally go back to the person or school who paid, using the original payment method where that is practical. Complimentary access involves no payment, so there is nothing to refund under this courtesy.

How to ask for help or a refund

Use the contact form or email support@studystrides.com.au and tell us, if you have them:

  • the student’s nickname or name, and the email used for the login
  • who paid (student, parent/guardian or school)
  • what happened and what you would like us to do

You do not need to be the signed-in student to contact us. Please give us enough detail so we can find the right payment or access record.

Where a refund is payable, it will normally go back to the person or school who paid, using the original payment method where that is practical.

Complimentary access involves no payment, so there is no payment to refund. If complimentary access was promised and not provided, we will correct that.

We will handle refund requests as soon as we reasonably can.

If we change or cannot continue the service

If we make a material change to the service we agreed to provide, or we cannot continue providing access through the purchased term, we will tell affected students or payers as soon as we reasonably can. You keep your Australian Consumer Law rights. Where the law requires a refund, or where you do not agree to a material change, we will provide the appropriate refund to the payer.

If access is ended for serious misuse

If we suspend or end access because a login was shared, used to copy the site materials for others, or used unlawfully, a change-of-mind refund will not usually apply. Your Australian Consumer Law rights still apply if the underlying problem was with our service rather than with that misuse.

If we cannot resolve a problem

Please contact us first. If we cannot resolve it, you can contact NSW Fair Trading for consumer help: nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/fair-trading.

The Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) accepts reports about consumer issues and uses them for education and enforcement. The ACCC generally does not resolve individual disputes.
