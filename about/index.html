About Us

StudyStrides is taught by highly experienced, passionate, and successful NSW Studies of Religion teachers. They bring exceptional subject knowledge and a clear understanding of how the HSC assesses Studies of Religion.

How students make strides

The work rests on deep learning, strategy, drill, and practice. Students learn the course so the ideas stay with them, then learn the strategy for using that knowledge in HSC responses. Drill and practice make the strategy reliable. Those steps become large strides toward academic success.
